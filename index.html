About Noodle Clash

Noodle Clash drops you into a giant noodle bowl as a short, hungry noodle. Eat glowing food to grow longer, boost to speed past rivals, and never let your head touch another noodle.

Cut across rival noodles so they crash into your body, then feast on the glowing orbs they leave behind. There are several modes, including a two-player mode on one screen.

3 quick tips

  1. Stay near the middle at first, away from the deadly border and the crowded crash zones.
  2. Leave space around your head. Most crashes happen when you squeeze through a gap that closes.
  3. Turn in wide curves. Sharp, panicked turns often put your head straight into a body.

How to play

🕹SteerJoystick: drag anywhere on the left side. Follow mode: your noodle chases your finger. Mouse: point. Keys: ← → or A D.
✨Eat glowing orbsEvery orb makes you longer and a bit thicker. Big glowing orbs come from crashed snakes.
⚡BoostHold the ⚡ button, a second finger, the mouse button, Space or ↑. Boosting burns length and drops orbs behind you.
💥Clash!Any snake whose head touches another body explodes into orbs. Cut in front of rivals, or coil around them to trap them. Avoid the red border.
🧠Know your rivalsHunters (🎯) chase heads and cut you off. Cowards (💨) flee big snakes. Encirclers (🌀) coil around smaller prey. Grazers (🍥) chase food.
🏆Earn coinsEarn coins from length, clashes, missions and achievements, then spend them on skins and patterns in the shop.
